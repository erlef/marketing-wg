Attendee Guide

Stockholm Unconference, May 19

Basic Details

Address is:

Viderögatan 3b, 164 40 Kista, Sweden

At our host Entryfy

We will start at 10:00. Feel free to come a bit before. We have not made arrangements for breakfast or snacks. We will be going out for lunch.

The Order

We will start with topic collection. This will happen in the shared topic spreadsheet. Feel free to open it at any time and put your topic ideas in.

The topic types are:

  • Teach something you know to others
  • Learn something you want to know that others may like to teach
  • Discuss a topic for open discussion, problem solving
  • Debate two sides of an issue that would be interesting to defend, argue for, convince others

After topics are in, each one will have 2 minutes to explain and answer questions.

We will then vote by hands on topics. Voting for more than one topic is allowed.

Based on the votes, we will start with 15 minute sessions through the order.

We will stop around 1200 and head out for lunch for about 90 minutes.
