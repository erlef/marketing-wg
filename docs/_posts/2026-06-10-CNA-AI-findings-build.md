Vulnerability reports in just April 2026 exceeded all of Q1. The
reporting **rate** is also climbing and it is driven by **AI**. There is no
short term relief expected. Can you help?

At this moment, the CNA volume spike is driven by friendly use of
AI. People, and only a few, in the community are using the latest
public models to discover vulnerabilities that have been sitting
undiscovered, for years in some cases.

This won't last we don't know how long but eventually unfriendly
agents will probe all parts of our ecosystem. We have a window of
opportunity to get the upper hand the more resources we put in now
harder it will be for unfriendly actors later. This is a resource
issue. This is what we're asking if you are a security researcher if
your company has security researchers, this is a great opportunity to
get involved in a collaborative effort in using these tools to protect
our ecosystem. If you do not have either of those funding is what we
need.


Published CVEs by the CNA is a late step in the process. 
We are capacity constrained. You can help. We need your help.


There's more now than ever and it is not slop

In just the month of April, our CNA exceeded reports of all Q1. So far, we are at 2x and on track to 4x. We expect to see the rate of ascent to continue climbing. The reports are AI driven, but not the slop of six months ago. These are real vulnerabilities and they impact the core components and the hex top 100.

Most of these recent reports came from one researcher, using public AI models and harness. If one researcher can achieve non-linear growth, adding many will have an effect hard to anticipate.
