  Ericsson has renewed their support for the EEF in 2026.
  In addition to all that Ericsson does, by operating the OTP team,
  embedding Erlang in their products, they also provide financial support
  for the EEF mission that supports the community and ecosystem around
  the technology they created. The EEF so appreciates Ericsson's ongoing
  commitment to Erlang and their support for our work.
