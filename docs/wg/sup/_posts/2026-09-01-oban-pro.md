[Oban Pro](https://oban.pro), offering and supporting [Oban](https://github.com/oban-bg), the most widely used Elixir queuing system, has become a sponsor of the EEF by providing financial support.
Oban Pro layers on the advanced features needed to scale complex background job systems, and Oban Web brings the whole cluster into one real-time dashboard.
