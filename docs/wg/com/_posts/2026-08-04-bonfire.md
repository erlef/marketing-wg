On July 27, [Bonfire](https://bonfirenetworks.org), a fediverse server
and client written in Elixir, announces it is focusing on tools to
build communities. In their
[post](https://bonfirenetworks.org/posts/bonfire-for-communities/),
they say that bonfire was meant to be more than another fediverse
implementation. It was always envisioned to be a tool and support for
organizing communities.

The EEF has been tracking the development of bonfire with great
interest and specifically around community tooling. We intend to
deploy it and discover if it can help our ecosystem. Let us know if
you're interested in getting involved.

Either way, check out Bonfire and see what they're up to.
