A good venue for an UnCon includes:

- a room with capacity of 30-50
- chairs, no tables
- large TV or projector with an HDMI or USB-C connector
- a small table or lectern for speakers
- time available from 9:30 to 4:30, event starts at 10:00, but there is always some setup
- optimally, a room that is sound isolated and lights can be controlled
- location near good spots for lunch
- good to have: venue allows bringing in food/snacks/drinks
