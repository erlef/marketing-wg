There are many neighborhoods in our community which make up the Erlang
Ecosystem. The challenge for the EEF is to foster awareness across the
community of interconnected work, research, problems, solutions
and our shared destiny. There is a threshold between collaborative
neighborhoods and social/technical fragmentation. The objective is to
foster the former and combat the latter.

To that end, the EEF Community Working Group (formerly Marketing
Working Group), has the primary mission to build interconnection
between components of the overall community.

# Plan

## Branding

Create and shepherd a unified brand that reuses visuals across all media accounts and eef sources

## Media strategy

Create a cross media presence and regular cadence of content that is
both interesting and advances the community cohesion goal.

### Topics

* Sponsor initiation, renewal, creating product/service awareness for mutual benefit
* Working group news
* Non-EEF events (date announcements, releasing schedules, releasing media)
* EEF community events
* Stipend milestones (start, releases, outcomes)
* Strategic and Visionary induction
* Security, packaging releases, notifications
* Web Article releases, interview releases
* Podcast announcements
* Some technical news (high profile, possibly reposting only to amplify)
* Event highlights, from meetups and others
* Industry events, collaboration, EEF representation at
* EEF Board elections
* EEF membership
* EEF volunteer profiles
* EEF stipend recipient videos

## Events

Build toward global stand-alone regional events that are community oriented, supported, and run.
Currently limited to major conference companion Unconferences.


## Annual General Meeting

Produce, collect year's events, topics, media for playback during AGM

## Web Strategy

Using unified branding, represent all topics as web accessible content
(media posts all backed by web content EEF owns or community owns via
other sources)

Develop better ways to integrate topics, media accounts (possibly pull
media posts into the web as well as organizing content by posting
calendar so long term discovery is possible). Federate community
sources through some kind of syndication that drives traffic to other
sites and EEF sites offer good entry point.

## Calendar, events

Double down on iCal publishing for all things:
* Events
* Meetings
* Releases
* Anniversaries

Maximize time federation and out-bound linkages.
