With the hex team's [just announced](https://hex.pm/blog/announcing-workload-identity-and-staged-publishing) general availability of Workload Identity, comes a new era of package security. It was previously not recommended to publish releases from CI, but now with Workload Identity CI publishing is the recommended approach. This is a major step forward in both securing package supply chain as well as lowering release process complexity for maintainers.
