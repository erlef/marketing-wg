---
title: Student Virtual Tickets 2026
description: EEF has become 2026 sponsors for ElixirConf/CodeBEAM to support student virtual tickets.
---
Continuing our support of student access to ecosystem conferences as introduction or deepening knowledge, the EEF has renewed sponsorship with CodeSync conferences to offer virtual student tickets.

Conferences tickets are available for:

- [ElixirConf US](https://elixirconf.com)
- [CodeBEAM Europe](https://codebeameurope.com)
