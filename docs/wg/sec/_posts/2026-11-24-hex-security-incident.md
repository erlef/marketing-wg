It's just another Tuesday (in the LLM security age), time to update your password and security tokens in your hex account at [https://hex.pm/dashboard/security](https://hex.pm/dashboard/security). This should be your first response to Hex reporting a security incident in [their blog](https://hex.pm/blog). The short version is, change your security tokens. Did I mention it is time to change your password? The key axis of exploit is not a vulnerability in any one component, but disjoint threat models across the combination of Protocols, Structs, binary to term, and trusting round trip data with authenticated users.

For subtlety, if you have a *strong* password on hex, you don't really need to change it. Mine is very long and strong, I still changed it and re-on-boarded my 2FA.
